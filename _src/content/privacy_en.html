The short version

  • This policy covers every app published by DNNR Tech on the App Store and Google Play. App-by-app details are at the bottom.
  • Most of our apps work without an account. Only TJ Near & Hot offers optional sign-in.
  • Our apps are free and show ads. Ads can use your device's advertising ID, which you can reset or limit at any time.
  • Some features send content you choose (for example, a menu photo or your resume) to Google's Gemini AI to produce a result.
  • We don't sell your personal information for money. You can ask us to delete your data at any time: hello@dnnr.us.

Who we are and what this covers

DNNR Tech ("we", "us") develops and publishes mobile apps. This Privacy Policy explains what information our apps collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It applies to all apps published under the developer name DNNR Tech, and to this website (dnnr.us). If an app needs to say more, the details are in App-by-app details.

Questions? Email hello@dnnr.us.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Content you create in apps with community features, such as comments, replies, recipes, lists, suggestions, reports, photos you attach, and a public nickname.
  • Content you ask an app to process, such as a photo of a menu or ingredients, a resume or CV, a job description, a search, or a destination.
  • Messages you send us through in-app feedback, forms, or email, along with basic details such as app version and device type.
  • Account details if you choose to sign in with Google or Apple in TJ Near & Hot (your email address, and your name if Apple shares it).

Information collected automatically

  • Identifiers: an anonymous app user ID, Firebase installation IDs, a randomly generated or hashed install ID in some apps, a push notification token if you allow notifications, and your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Advertising ID on Android).
  • Usage and diagnostics: which screens and features you use, app performance, and crash reports.
  • Device information: device model, operating system, language, time zone, and app version.

Location

Several apps use your location, with your permission, to show nearby places or distances. Most use it only while you're using the app. AutoMiles uses location in the background, if you allow it, so it can record your drives automatically; your trips stay on your device. Where an app sends location to our servers or stores it, we say so in its app details.

Photos, camera, and files

Apps only access photos, videos, the camera, or files when you use a feature that needs them. StampRescue and Golf Passport can scan the location and date information in your photo library to find places you've visited. This scan runs on your device, and your photos are not uploaded. Photos you attach to a post or report are uploaded so others can see them.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple or Google. We never receive your card details. Apps with purchases use RevenueCat to confirm what you bought.

How we use information

  • To provide app features, such as finding nearby places, recording trips, translating menus, or tailoring a resume
  • To run community features, including showing your posts to others and moderating content for safety
  • To remember your settings and sync data you've chosen to share, such as visits or saved places
  • To understand how apps are used, fix bugs, and improve performance
  • To show ads and measure how they perform
  • To protect our services from abuse and fraud (for example, with Firebase App Check)
  • To respond to your messages and requests
  • To comply with law and enforce our terms

AI features

Some apps use Google's Gemini AI models. When you use these features, the app sends the content needed for the task to Google, which returns the result:

  • What to Eat: menu photos, recognized text, and dish names
  • Academic CV AI and Targeted Resume AI: your resume or CV text and the job or program description you provide
  • Snap & Shake: photos of your drinks or ingredients
  • Time Zone Alarm: the city or time zone you search for
  • TJ Near & Hot, Secret Menu for Super Duper, and BapMap: posts and images submitted to the community, for automatic moderation (and translation in BapMap)

Google processes this content under its own terms and privacy policy. Resumes and CVs often contain personal details, so remove anything you don't want processed, such as a phone number or home address, before uploading. Swing Like Pro analyzes your swing videos on your device, and they are not uploaded.

Advertising

Our apps show ads through Google AdMob. Some apps also use these ad partners through AdMob mediation: Meta Audience Network, Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), Liftoff Monetize (Vungle), and Pangle. Ad partners may collect your advertising identifier, device information, and ad interactions to show and measure ads, and in some cases to personalize them.

  • iOS: apps that personalize ads ask for permission first (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalized using your IDFA.
  • Android: you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Some apps show only non-personalized ads. Some apps show Google's consent form to users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland.

  • Business transfers, if an app or DNNR Tech is sold or merged, under this policy's protections

How long we keep information

  • Data kept only on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Account data and content you post are kept until you delete them, delete your account, or ask us to delete them.
  • Feedback messages are kept as long as needed to respond and improve our apps.
  • Analytics, crash, and advertising data are kept according to our providers' retention settings. For example, Firebase Analytics keeps event data for up to 14 months.

Your choices and rights

  • Permissions: you can turn off location, photos, camera, notifications, or tracking at any time in your device settings. Some features may stop working.
  • Deletion: see Data & account deletion. TJ Near & Hot has an in-app Delete account option. StampRescue and Golf Passport have Delete my shared data.
  • Access and correction: you can ask for a copy of your data or ask us to correct it.
  • Regional rights: depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK, California, or Korea), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email hello@dnnr.us. We respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm the request came from you. Because most of our apps don't use accounts, it helps to include the app name and any details that help us find your data.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and international transfers

We use reasonable safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls, and abuse protection. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our apps change. We will post the new version here and update the effective date. If a change is significant, we will provide additional notice, for example in the app.

{{app:tj-near-hot}}
