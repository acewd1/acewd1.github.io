Information we collect

  • Information you provide: posts, photos, and a nickname you share in community features; content you ask an app to process, such as a photo, document text, search, or address; and messages you send us through an app, by email, or through our website (such as your name, email address, and message).
  • Account information: some apps use an anonymous account or offer optional sign-in with Google or Apple. If you sign in, we receive your email address and, if Apple shares it, your name.
  • Information collected automatically: identifiers such as an app user ID, install ID, push token, and your device's advertising ID; usage, performance, and crash data; device model, operating system, language, time zone, and app version; and your IP address and the approximate location derived from it. This is collected through analytics and advertising tools (SDKs) in our apps. Our website may use cookies needed for security and operation; you can block cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work.
  • Location: with your permission, to find nearby places or calculate distances and routes. Some apps use it only on your device; others send it to a server when a feature needs it. Some apps, if you allow it, use location and motion in the background (for example, to record drives automatically).
  • Photos, camera, and files: accessed only when you use a feature that needs them. Photo location and date data and video analysis are processed on your device where possible. Some apps send the result, such as which city or place a photo matched, to our analytics. Only photos you post, attach, or send to an AI feature are uploaded.
  • Purchases: payments are handled by Apple or Google. We never receive your card details, only the purchase records needed to confirm what you bought.

Some apps keep records, such as health or vaccination records, only on your device and don't send them to our servers.

  • To understand how apps are used, fix bugs, and improve performance
  • To show ads and measure how they perform
  • To protect our services from abuse and keep them secure
  • To respond to your messages and comply with law

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example, in the EU/UK or Korea), we rely on providing the service you request, your consent (for location, photos, notifications, and tracking), our legitimate interests (analytics, security, and improving our apps), and our legal obligations.

Google processes this content under its own terms and privacy policy, so remove anything you don't want processed, such as a phone number or home address, before you send it.

YouTube API Services

We use YouTube API Services in an internal tool that uploads and manages videos on our own YouTube channels (TwosomeVoyage, Hyatt Life Globalist, US Roadtrip 360, US Roadtrip POV, and Useful Little Apps) and reads those channels' analytics. Only DNNR Tech staff use this tool; it does not access or collect information from viewers or other YouTube users. By using it we are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles YouTube data under the Google Privacy Policy.

  • What it accesses: our channels' videos, video details (title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy status), and channel analytics.
  • What we store: an authorization token per channel, kept only on our own computer and never shared, plus our videos' IDs and analytics reports. We don't sell or share this data.
  • Revoking and deleting: access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. When access is revoked or the tool is no longer used, we delete the stored tokens and any YouTube data we kept within 30 days. You can also ask us to delete data by writing to {{email}}.

Advertising

Mozzi (toddler photo play)

Mozzi is made for children under 13 (around ages 2 to 5) and is set up by a parent. It handles much less information than our other apps, so for Mozzi this section applies instead of the general sections above.

  • Photos, drawings and sentences stay on the device. The family photos you choose, the album you pick, the short sentence read for each photo (including sentences the app writes on the device), and your child's drawings are stored only on the device. Mozzi doesn't upload them, and we never receive them. Mozzi reads photos only from the album or selection you make on the parent screen.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase). To see how Mozzi is used and improve it, the app sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase: for example, that a play session started and how long it lasted, which photo theme was opened, how many photos were wiped, and whether the parent screen was opened. These events are tied to a random app-instance ID that Google assigns to the installation, and include the app version, device model, operating system, language, and the approximate region estimated from the IP address. They never include photos, file names, the sentences about photos, drawings, recordings, names, or anything you or your child type.
  • No advertising ID, no ads. Mozzi doesn't request or send the Android advertising ID, and ad personalization in Analytics is turned off. Mozzi shows no ads and has no purchases, accounts or sign-in.
  • Permissions. Photo access, to show the photos you choose; on Android, optional Do Not Disturb access, to quiet other apps' alerts during play. Mozzi doesn't use location, camera, microphone or contacts.
  • Children's privacy. We collect the usage statistics described above only to operate and improve the app, not to build profiles or to target advertising. We don't knowingly collect any other personal information from children. A parent can ask us to delete the analytics data linked to their installation by writing to {{email}} with the app name; uninstalling Mozzi also stops all collection.
  • Retention. Google Analytics keeps user-level event data for 14 months; aggregated reports may be kept longer.
