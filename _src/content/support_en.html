Contact us

Send us your question, feedback, or bug report. We read every message and usually reply within a few business days.

To help us respond faster, please include:

  • The app name
  • Your device and OS version (for example, iPhone 15 / iOS 18, or Pixel 8 / Android 15)
  • The app version (usually in the app's settings or about screen)
  • What happened, and a screenshot if possible

Send a message

You can also email hello@dnnr.us.

Request deletion of your data

You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

  • Apps with an account: use the Delete account option in the app's settings, if available, or email us.
  • Apps without an account: data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. To delete any data we hold on our servers, email us.

Send a request with the app name using the button below, or email hello@dnnr.us with the subject "Data deletion request".

Request data deletion

See Data & account deletion for what is deleted, what may be kept, and how long it takes.

Purchases and refunds

In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google. To manage a subscription or request a refund, use your App Store or Google Play account. We can't access your payment details.
